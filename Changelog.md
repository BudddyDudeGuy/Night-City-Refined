# Changelog

All notable changes to Night City Refined are listed here. Builds are published on the [Releases](https://github.com/BudddyDudeGuy/Cyberpunk_2077-Modlist/releases) page.

## v1.0.0 - 2026-09-21

Initial release.

- 513 mods, 537 archives to download, 6.91 GB of free space needed.
- Built from the `Weapons Improved` profile.
- Includes ReShade (RenoDX HDR), bundled with the list so there is nothing extra to download or configure.
- Hardware-dependent mods ship disabled under the `READ TO ENABLE OR DISABLE BASED ON YOUR SETUP` separator in Mod Organizer 2. Enable the ones that match your setup before first launch.
