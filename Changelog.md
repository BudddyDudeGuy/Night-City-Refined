## Version 1.1.6

![Save Compatible](https://img.shields.io/badge/Save-Compatible-brightgreen) ![Released](https://img.shields.io/badge/Released-October_6,_2026-lightgrey)
> Netrunning now follows one rule: free to look, earned to hurt.

#### Gameplay
- Removed Dead Body Alert ([link](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/26746)): Much Better AI already handles finding bodies, and it checks line of sight instead of noticing bodies through walls.
- Removed Replace Weapon Mods ([link](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/15409)).
- Added UnequipMods - Remove Weapon ([link](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/31701)): weapon mods can be taken off any weapon, and they come back to your inventory when you sell, dismantle or drop a gun. Press Period (.) or LB + D-pad Up in a weapon's mod screen to strip every mod at once.
- Updated Much Better AI ([link](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/31402)): enemies flank, punish your reloads and heals, hack a little more often, and hold out longer before breaking and retreating.
- Changed: cameras and basic devices (doors, vending machines and the like) can always be hacked without a breach. Turrets and hacks on people still need one.
- Changed: failed breaches no longer punish you, so retrying is free.
- Changed: you get traced far less. Enemy netrunners only fight back if you fail a breach while one is on the network or hack right next to one who is already alert. Hacking things that are not on a network no longer alerts them, mechs and drones can no longer trace you, and hacking through cameras, turrets or RC cars follows the same trace rules as hacking in person.
- Changed: enemy netrunners stay hidden from Ping, so you have to find them yourself.
- Changed: enemy gunfire hits at full damage again. Their accuracy stays slightly lowered so you are not beamed across the map.
- Changed: explosions only knock you down when you are close to the blast.
- Changed: Elites can only be stunned with a gun butt below 20% health. Bosses still can't be stunned.
- Changed: picking up a body no longer forces your gun out.
- Changed: iconic weapon looks can no longer be crafted, so iconics stay special.

- Added Finisher Grace Fix ([link](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/34456)): enemies no longer survive killing blows at 1 HP over and over.
- Added Machete Finisher Kill Fix ([link](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/34454)): machete finishers kill instead of knocking out.
- Added Melee Attack Speed Cap Fix ([link](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/34588)): melee attack speed above 200% now counts.

- Fixed dead enemies sometimes showing on the minimap as live enemies with vision cones.
- Fixed the Vehicle Dismiss keyboard key, which was set to a controller button. Hold R on keyboard, D-pad Up on controller.

#### Miscellaneous
- The profiles are now named by PC tier and listed low to high: **1 - Low-End PC** (no ray tracing), **2 - Mid-Range PC** (ray tracing, the default) and **3 - High-End PC** (path tracing). Only the names changed.
- Cleaner optional section in MO2: the lighting mods now sit with the other lighting mods (the profiles switch them), and the plain Dirt Begone is on by default. Switch to the DLSS or FSR version in `READ TO ENABLE OR DISABLE BASED ON YOUR SETUP` if you use one.
- Crash reports from Advanced Crash Reporter are in your install folder under `overwrite\bin\x64\plugins\AdvancedCrashReporter\reports` (the 1.1.4 notes said the game folder, which was wrong).

## Version 1.1.4

![Save Compatible](https://img.shields.io/badge/Save-Compatible-brightgreen) ![Released](https://img.shields.io/badge/Released-October_3,_2026-lightgrey)
> Three profiles, so the lighting matches your graphics settings.

#### Gameplay
- Changed: quickhacks no longer have a distance limit once you breach a network. You still need to breach first, except for the quickhacks that never need a breach (Ping, Whistle, Distract and Covert hacks).

#### Visuals
- Added The Grid (Custom Street Light Hours - Weather Effects - Blackouts) ([link](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/34592)): street lights come on at 6 PM and off at 6 AM, earlier in rain, fog and overcast weather, and they light up across the city in a wave instead of all at once.

#### Fixes & QoL
- Updated Advanced Crash Reporter ([link](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/34403)): it now loads in game, and after a crash the game closes on its own, so Steam no longer gets stuck on "game still running".

#### Miscellaneous
- Added three profiles. Pick one from the profile dropdown at the top of MO2. **Raster** (no ray tracing or path tracing) turns off the lighting overhauls, which fixes the game looking far too dark without ray tracing. **Ray Tracing** (the default) keeps Nova City 2 and The Nullifier. **Path Tracing** turns on ReLUX, ReLUX Plus, Cyberpunk Ultra Skin and the path tracing version of Cargo Lights.
- MO2 now shows which version of Night City Refined you have at the top of the mod list.
- The empty DISABLED section at the bottom of MO2 is now called `ADD YOUR OWN MODS HERE`. Mods you install yourself land there.
