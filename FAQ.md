# Leveling, Attributes and Perks

>[!WARNING]
>**Spend all of your attribute points in character creation.** Whatever you put into each attribute there becomes its permanent starting value. Any points you leave unspent are lost for good. You cannot spend them later.

<Details>
<summary><b>How does leveling work in this list?</b></summary>
<br>

You level up by doing things, not by picking from a menu.

The game has five skills, and each one is tied to one attribute:

| Skill | Raises this attribute |
|---|---|
| Solo | Body |
| Shinobi | Reflexes |
| Headhunter | Cool |
| Netrunner | Intelligence |
| Engineer | Technical Ability |

Every time a skill goes up, the attribute tied to it goes up with it, automatically. The normal attribute points you get from leveling up are turned off. The only way to raise an attribute is to use its skill.

So the more you play a certain way, the better you get at it. If you spend the whole game with a shotgun, your Body goes up. If you hack everything, your Intelligence goes up.

You can see your skill levels in the Attributes screen. Click `Skill Progression` in the bottom right corner.

</Details>

<Details>
<summary><b>What levels up each skill?</b></summary>
<br>

**Solo (Body)**
- Killing enemies with shotguns, LMGs, hammers, clubs, your fists and Gorilla Arms

**Shinobi (Reflexes)**
- Killing enemies with assault rifles, SMGs, katanas, machetes, chainswords and Mantis Blades
- Running, jumping, climbing, dodging and dashing

**Headhunter (Cool)**
- Killing enemies with pistols, revolvers, precision rifles, knives and axes
- Headshots with any weapon
- Kills with silenced weapons, and takedowns

**Netrunner (Intelligence)**
- Using quickhacks on enemies and objects. Killing an enemy with a quickhack is worth more
- Jacking into access points
- Killing enemies while locked on with a smart weapon

**Engineer (Technical Ability)**
- Crafting items
- Disassembling items
- Killing enemies with charged tech weapons, grenades and the Projectile Launch System
- Using the Overload quickhack
- Doing elemental damage and damage over time, like burning, shock and poison

</Details>

<Details>
<summary><b>What should I do with my points in character creation?</b></summary>
<br>

Put them into whatever fits the V you want to play. That is your starting point, and you level up from there as you play.

Spend every point. Points you leave unspent in character creation are gone for good, because the normal attribute points are turned off in this list. There is no menu to spend them in later.

Starting points are a head start, not a limit. Every attribute can still reach 20 by leveling its skill to the max, no matter where it started.

</Details>

<Details>
<summary><b>I leveled up but I can't spend my perk point. Is something broken?</b></summary>
<br>

No. Perks are locked behind attribute levels. The first row of perks in each tree needs that attribute at **4**. The next rows need **9**, **15** and **20**.

Attributes start at 3. If you did not put any points into an attribute in character creation, none of that tree's perks can be bought yet.

Your perk point is not lost. It stays saved until you can use it. Level any skill up to rank 3, which only takes a few kills, and its attribute will reach 4. Then you can spend the point in that tree.

</Details>

<Details>
<summary><b>How do I get perk points?</b></summary>
<br>

- One perk point every time your character levels up, the same as the base game.
- One extra perk point for every 20 levels in any skill.

Character level and Street Cred go up a little slower than in the base game. Skill XP comes in fast early on and slows down as the skill gets close to its max.

</Details>
